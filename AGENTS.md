## Portfolio architecture
- Keep the portfolio as a single content route with anchor-linked chapters; all requested sections belong to the same personal narrative.
- Keep social URLs and contact details unlinked until supplied; never infer personal account identities from a name.
- Label proposed projects and learning interests accurately rather than presenting them as verified achievements.
- Use shared semantic styling in src/styles.css and the existing Button component for interactive controls.
